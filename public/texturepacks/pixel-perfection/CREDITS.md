# Pixel Perfection

By Hugh "XSSheep" Rutland. Contributors: Toby109tt, tacotexmex, devurandom.

Source: https://github.com/minetest-texture-packs/Pixel-Perfection

License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (see `LICENSE.txt`).

BunkCraft uses a subset of these textures unmodified. At runtime, grass and leaves textures
are converted to greyscale and the green overlay of the grass block side is masked, so they
can be tinted per biome. Water uses the first frame of the animation strip. Ore and
grass-side textures are composited from the pack's base and overlay images.
